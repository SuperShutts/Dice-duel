How to play

Both players start with 20 HP. You attack first, then take turns. Both roll a six-sided die each turn. Damage is attack − defense. A tie or a higher defense blocks the attack.
Attack 5 − defense 2 = 3 damage.
Bring your rival to 0 HP to win.

Fair dice. Fresh chances. May the best roll win.
